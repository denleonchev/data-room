Privacy Policy

Bonadev Data Room · last updated 16 August 2026

Bonadev Data Room is a virtual data room for due diligence: you upload documents, organise them into folders, and share them with people you choose. This page explains what the service stores about you and what happens to it.

What is collected

  • Account details — your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, those come from your Google profile, along with your profile picture. No other Google data is requested.
  • Your content — the files you upload and the names of the folders you create.
  • A session cookie — set when you sign in, so the service recognises you on the next request. It is not readable by JavaScript.

What is not collected

There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Your data is never sold, and it is never used to train anything.

Where it is stored

Account records and file metadata live in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase; uploaded files live in Supabase Storage in a private bucket. The backend runs on Railway, the frontend on Vercel. Data is stored in the European Union.

Who can see your files

Everything you upload is private to your account by default. It becomes visible to someone else only when you share it: either through a link you create, which anyone holding it can open, or by granting access to specific people. You can revoke either at any time, and access ends immediately.

Deleting your data

Deleting a file or folder removes it from the service. To delete your account and everything in it, write to den.leonchev@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Contact
